Privacy Policy for EgyHub

Last Updated: October 3, 2026

ROSIVA LLC ("we", "us", or "our") operates the EgyHub mobile application ("EgyHub" or the "App").

This Privacy Policy explains how EgyHub handles information when you use the application.

1. Information We Collect

EgyHub is an informational tourism and discovery application.

The App does not require users to create an account or provide personal information to use its main features.

EgyHub does not intentionally collect, store, or process:

  • Names
  • Email addresses from users
  • Phone numbers
  • Passwords
  • User accounts
  • GPS or precise location data
  • User-uploaded photos or files
  • Comments or reviews
  • Notification data
  • Advertising identifiers for advertising purposes

EgyHub does not provide user registration or login functionality.

2. Location Information

EgyHub does not request or collect the user's GPS location or precise device location.

The App may display the location of tourist attractions, museums, hotels, restaurants, or other places for informational purposes.

When the user chooses to open a location in Google Maps, the App may open the Google Maps application or a related Google Maps webpage. Any information handled by Google Maps is governed by Google's own privacy policies and terms.

EgyHub does not receive or store the user's GPS location as a result of opening Google Maps.

3. How We Use Information

Because EgyHub does not collect personal user information, we do not use personal information for profiling, advertising, marketing, or user tracking.

The App is primarily designed to display tourism-related information and help users discover places and related information.

4. Third-Party Services and Links

EgyHub may provide links or actions that direct users to external services, including Google Maps.

These external services are operated independently by their respective providers. Once you leave EgyHub and interact with an external service, that service's own privacy policy and terms apply.

EgyHub does not control and is not responsible for the privacy practices, content, availability, or data handling practices of third-party services.

5. Data Sharing

EgyHub does not sell, rent, or share users' personal information because the App does not intentionally collect personal information from users.

If a user voluntarily interacts with an external third-party service through a link or button in the App, that interaction is subject to the third party's own privacy practices.

6. Data Security

Since EgyHub does not intentionally collect or maintain personal user information, there is no user personal-data database maintained by the App for account or profile purposes.

We nevertheless take reasonable measures to maintain the security and integrity of the application.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

EgyHub does not maintain personal user accounts or intentionally store personal information submitted by users.

As a result, there is no personal account data that users need to request deletion of.

Information displayed by the application may be updated, modified, or removed as the application and its content are maintained.

8. Children's Privacy

EgyHub does not knowingly collect personal information from children or require children to provide personal information to use the application.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the application, applicable requirements, or our privacy practices.

Any updated version will be made available through the applicable privacy policy page.
